Social Calendar

One of the reasons why we celebrate our wedding in Menorca is because we want to enjoy the island with you all. We know that some of you will be here a few days before the wedding. We are going to organise some activities and we hope you can join us.

Monday 16th - Sailing trip

We will rent a sailboat to get to some of the most hidden beaches, swim, and do some snorkelling. More information at Menorca a Vela's website

Tuesday 18 - Beach

We will pick a beach to spend the day. It's recommended to bring some drinks and food as usually there are no services around.

Wednesday 18 - To the Town Hall!

We will get legally married at 12 pm at Ciudatella's Town Hall. After that we will celebrate with a meal and some drinks. We would love you to join us.

Tursday 19 - Beach

We will pick a beach to spend the day. It's recommended to bring some drinks and food as usually there are no services around.

Friday 20 - Pre-wedding

Since 8pm until 10.30pm we will have some beers and cold dinner at the venue, in Toraixa. Time to meet everybody. This night cannot get out of control, so at around 10:30pm we will start asking everyone to leave.
